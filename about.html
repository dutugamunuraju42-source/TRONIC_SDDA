About TRONIC SDDA

Our Story

Founded by passionate electronics engineers, TRONIC SDDA began with a clear mission: to bridge the gap between complex hardware ideas and tangible, reliable electronic products. What started as a small workshop for local prototyping has grown into a comprehensive design and fabrication hub.

We believe that hardware is hard, but it shouldn't be impossible. By providing professional PCB design, reliable fabrication, and an accessible component shop, we empower makers, startups, and large enterprises to innovate faster and more efficiently.
